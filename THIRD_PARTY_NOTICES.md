# Third-party viewer components

This module integrates, without modifying their source repositories:

- Zrimo `@zrimo/viewer` 0.1.2 — MIT OR Apache-2.0 — https://github.com/bnku/zrimo
- ShrimpDocViewer `shrimp-doc-viewer` 0.5.0 — MIT OR Apache-2.0 — https://github.com/cjo4m06/shrimp-doc-viewer

The build script copies the license and third-party notice files shipped by the installed npm packages into `ViewerDist/licenses` when available.
